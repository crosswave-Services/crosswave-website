Core Division 01
Principal Division

NAFDAC & Regulatory Consultation

Professional guidance and coordination for businesses navigating product registration, regulatory documentation, import permits, compliance support and laboratory analysis processes.

Bringing a food, cosmetic, herbal or haircare product to market involves several procedural steps and documentation requirements. Our regulatory consultation service helps clients understand what applies to their product, prepare the right documentation and coordinate each stage of the application process.

We act as an independent consultant and coordinator on our clients' behalf, keeping them informed and organised throughout the engagement.

  • Food Product Registration Guidance
  • Cosmetics & Personal Care Registration Guidance
  • Herbal & Haircare Product Registration Guidance
  • Regulatory Documentation Support
  • Application Process Coordination
  • Import Permit Assistance
  • Regulatory Compliance Support
  • Laboratory Analysis Coordination
Important: Regulatory consultation and coordination services are provided to support clients through applicable processes and documentation requirements. Final registration, approvals, permits and regulatory decisions remain subject to the relevant authorities and their applicable procedures. CROSSWAVE is an independent consultancy and is not NAFDAC or any government agency.
